Access control runs across the structures

Authority is assigned through access records and checked by application/database rules—not inferred from a job title or a territory appointment.

1Person relationship — organization + profile + relationship type/status; may carry an organization permission role and department scope.
2Organization permission role — bundles catalogued permissions and scope: all, department, assigned, or own. Seeded Editor/Moderator permission roles are distinct from identically named legacy account profiles.
3Capability check — enabled module, active relationship/role, permission, scope, and applicable HR policy readiness are checked.

Employee assignments reference an organization permission role. Some newsroom operations still check fixed legacy account profiles too; therefore these similarly named role records are not yet one unified permission model. Do not treat either role as a job title.

What is a real hierarchy?

  • Geographic locations: State → Region → Zone → Range → District → Coverage area. The database validates these parent levels; legacy city rows may remain.
  • HR departments: departments can nest under other departments. Positions belong to a department.
  • HR reporting: each employee assignment can identify a manager employee; this is a manager link, not an automatically derived org chart.
  • Organization: a primary organization scopes its configuration and people relationships; the organization root is not the parent of every profile row.

What the system does not imply

  • A department-head appointment is not inferred from a job title or manager relationship.
  • A geographic leadership appointment does not grant application permissions.
  • Reporter coverage does not appoint a territory leader.
  • A staff title does not create a new account access profile.
  • Optional catalog entries such as payroll, recruitment, leave/attendance, and performance are not implemented modules just because they appear in the module catalog.
